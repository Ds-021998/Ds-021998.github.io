HabitAct

VR language game, solo project, Meta Quest 3S

A guide says "Prends le livre" and you answer by picking up the book. Three prototypes and 15 playtests turned a labelled room into a game where the action is the answer.

  • Attempts lost to the controls fell from 8 in 25 to none
  • All four final testers spoke French aloud while playing

Proofolio

Marker feedback usually disappears once the next assignment starts. I led the business model and requirements, drove the pivot to a verified record of student skills, and co-built the working app.

  • 18-page Django app with zero WCAG AA violations
